## Get Started in Three Simple Steps

Follow these easy steps to start using INEERO POS and manage your shop smoothly.

### Step 1: Install and Open INEERO POS

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="download" title="Download the POS">
    Download the application for your platform (Windows, Mac, or Android APK)
    from the official link provided.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="desktop" title="Install the POS">
    1. Run the installer and follow the instructions. 2. Once installed, open
    the POS app from your desktop or start menu.
    <Tip>You can create a shortcut for quick access to INEERO POS.</Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Set Up Your Shop

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="cog" title="Configure Shop Details">
    Enter your shop name, contact info, currency, and logo in the Settings
    section.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="box-open" title="Add Your First Products">
    1. Go to the Products section. 2. Click "Add Product" and enter the name,
    price, category, and stock. 3. Save to make the product available for sales.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Start Selling

<Accordion icon="cash-register" title="Use the POS Terminal">
  1. Open the POS Terminal from the dashboard. 2. Select products, add them to
  the sale, and process the payment. 3. Print or share receipts with your
  customers instantly.
</Accordion>
