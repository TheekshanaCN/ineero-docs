## Get Started in Three Easy Steps

Follow these simple steps to start using your POS and manage your shop efficiently.

### Step 1: Install and Open Your POS

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="download" title="Download the POS">
    Download the application for your platform (Windows, Mac, or Android APK)
    from the official link provided.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="desktop" title="Install the POS">
    1. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions. 2. Once
    installed, open the POS app from your desktop or start menu.
    <Tip>You can create a shortcut for quick access to your POS.</Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Set Up Your Shop

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="cog" title="Configure your shop details">
    Enter your shop name, contact info, currency, and logo in the settings
    section.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="box-open" title="Add your first products">
    1. Go to the Products section. 2. Click "Add Product" and enter the product
    name, price, category, and stock quantity. 3. Save to make the product
    available for sales.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Start Selling

<Accordion icon="cash-register" title="Use the POS Terminal">
  1. Open the POS Terminal from the dashboard. 2. Select products, add them to
  the sale, and process the payment. 3. Print or share receipts with your
  customers instantly.
</Accordion>
