---

<Info>
  **Requirements**:
  - Windows 10 or higher, MacOS, or Android device
  - At least 2 GB RAM and 500 MB free storage
</Info>

Follow these steps to install and run your POS application.

<Steps>
<Step title="Download the POS">

Download the installer for your platform (Windows, Mac, or Android APK) from the official link provided by your POS provider.

</Step>

<Step title="Install the POS">

1. Run the installer file and follow the on-screen instructions.  
2. Once installed, open the POS app from your desktop, start menu, or mobile device.

<Tip>Create a shortcut on your desktop for quick access.</Tip>
</Step>

<Step title="Initial Setup">

1. Open the POS and log in using your credentials.  
2. Set your shop name, contact information, and currency in the settings section.  
3. Add your first products to start selling.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='POS app not opening'>
    - Make sure your device meets the minimum system requirements.  
    - Try restarting your computer or mobile device.  
    - Reinstall the application if the problem persists.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Unable to log in">
    - Check that your username and password are correct.  
    - If you forgot your password, use the "Reset Password" option.  
    - Contact support if the issue continues.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Need Help?

If you encounter any other issues, visit the [FAQ & Support](./faq) section or contact support directly from the POS app.
